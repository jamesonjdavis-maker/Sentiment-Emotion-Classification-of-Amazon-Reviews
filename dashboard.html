Review Sentiment Audit

An LLM reads each review’s title and text — never its star rating — and labels the sentiment and primary emotion. This page shows how often it agrees with the reviewer’s stars, where it doesn’t, and how its emotions compare with a word-list method.

Primary emotion: LLM vs. word list

Two independent takes. The LLM names one emotion per review; the word list counts matches in the NRC Emotion Lexicon and takes the highest. “Tie” = the top emotions were triggered by the same word, so no single answer exists; “none” = no lexicon word matched.
